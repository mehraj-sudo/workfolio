Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2026

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly what data we collect and why.

TL;DR: WorkFolio stores your data locally in your own browser. We don't sell your data, don't track you with ads, and don't share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to provide the Service.
1

Who We Are

WorkFolio Suite ("we", "us", "our") operates the WorkFolio platform — a free resume builder, portfolio maker, and ATS scanner — accessible at workfolio.app. For privacy questions, contact us at support@workfolio.app.

3

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the WorkFolio Service.
  • To authenticate your account and restore your session.
  • To save your resumes and portfolios across sessions.
  • To respond to support messages sent via the Contact form.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond providing the Service.

4

Local Storage & Cookies

WorkFolio uses your browser's localStorage to store all your data locally. This data never leaves your device unless you enable Supabase cloud sync. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

We use a single functional item in sessionStorage to maintain your login session during a browsing session. This is cleared when you close the browser tab.

5

Third-Party Processors

  • Supabase (optional cloud sync) — stores your account and resume data when configured. Data is encrypted at rest. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Web3Forms — receives contact form submissions (name, email, message). See Web3Forms Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare CDN — may serve static assets (Font Awesome). No personal data is shared.
  • Google Fonts — may log IP addresses as part of font delivery. See Google Privacy Policy.
6

Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before storage — never stored as plain text.
  • No sensitive data is transmitted to WorkFolio servers (we have none by default).
  • The admin panel is PIN-protected with hashed comparison.
  • Supabase connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
7

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Delete — clear all your data by clearing your browser's localStorage or using the Admin panel's "Clear All Data" function.
  • Correct — update your account information at any time.
  • Portability — export your data as JSON via the Admin panel.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the Service and clear your data at any time.

For requests related to Supabase-stored data, contact us at support@workfolio.app and we will respond within 30 days.

8

Children's Privacy

WorkFolio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The Effective Date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of WorkFolio after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
